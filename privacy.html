Privacy policy

Finance Automation is a personal tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

  • What it accesses: the owner's own Google Sheets, Google Drive files, and read-only access to the owner's Gmail (to find bill amounts and due dates).
  • How data is used: only to update the owner's own spreadsheet. Gmail is never modified: nothing is sent, deleted, moved or labelled.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared with, or disclosed to anyone.
  • Storage: data stays in the owner's own Google account.
